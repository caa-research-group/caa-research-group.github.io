CAA Research Group

Helping people by keeping them informed.

Our goal is simple: to help people, by informing them. We're building an open, sourced record of news and information on every subject, including stories the news cycle drops, topics written off as fringe, and posts that get deleted. Each entry keeps its source link, a saved copy of its text and the date, so it can't quietly vanish and you can judge it for yourself.

Our standards

  • Every entry is sourced. Each one links to where it came from, and we rely primarily on original sources.
  • Everything is dated. You can see when each entry was posted and when it was added.
  • Nothing quietly disappears. We keep a copy of each entry's text, so it stays on the record even if the original is deleted.
  • No topic is left out. Political, contested or niche subjects are recorded the same way as everything else: neutrally, with their sources, so you can weigh them yourself.
  • Uncertainty is labeled. When evidence is thin or disputed, we say so.
  • A person reviews the work. AI helps with the research; a human signs off.

About

CAA Research Group was founded in October 2026, and we're just getting started. Our working prototype already holds about 3,500 entries across 39 topics, from politics and current events to science, health and technology, each with its source and date.

We use Claude to find, summarize, tag and cross-check entries, and a person reviews them.

Our second project: how-to guides, because life doesn't come with a manual. Practical, step-by-step help for the everyday things nobody ever taught you.
